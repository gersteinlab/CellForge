Develop a predictive model that estimates the gene expression profile of individual
K562 cells following CRISPR interference (CRISPRi), using the dataset from Adamson et
al. (2016, Cell).

Task definition:
- Input: the baseline gene expression profile of an unperturbed K562 cell, together
  with the identity of the target gene knocked down by CRISPRi.
- Output: the predicted log-normalised gene expression profile of that cell after
  perturbation, across all measured genes.

Evaluation scenarios:
1. Unseen perturbations — predict the effect of target genes that were entirely absent
   from training.
2. Unseen cell contexts — predict responses in cells whose baseline expression profiles
   were not observed during training.

Metrics, reported both genome-wide and restricted to differentially expressed genes:
- MSE — mean squared error between predicted and observed expression.
- PCC — Pearson correlation between predicted and observed profiles.
- R2 — proportion of observed variance explained by the prediction.

Differentially expressed genes are defined per perturbation by a Wilcoxon rank-sum test
against control cells with Benjamini-Hochberg correction, keeping genes with adjusted
p < 0.05 and |log2 fold change| > 0.5.

Additional constraints:
- Average metrics per perturbation, not per cell, so that heavily sampled perturbations
  do not dominate the score.
- Cell-state and batch variation must not leak into the perturbation representation;
  the model has to compose a perturbation onto a cell context it has not seen paired
  with that perturbation before.
- Report a comparison against at least the unperturbed-profile control baseline, so it
  is clear the model beats predicting no change.
